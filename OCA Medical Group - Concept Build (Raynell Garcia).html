Welcome

Welcome to OCA Medical Group

OCA Medical Group delivers integrated, patient-focused care through fellowship-trained physicians in pain management, orthopedic surgery and spine neurosurgery, supported by chiropractic and physical medicine.

As a multidisciplinary one-stop clinic, OCA offers on-site diagnostics and an attached ambulatory surgery center, so most patients go home the same day. The focus is long-term relief without reliance on addictive medication or unnecessary surgery.
